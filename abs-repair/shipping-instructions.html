Shipping instructions

Follow these steps after you purchase a repair service. Good packaging protects your module and keeps turnaround on schedule.

Before you shipPackagingWhat to includeWhere to send itAfter you ship

Before you ship

  • Have your order number. It is in your order confirmation email. Modules that arrive without an order number are held until we can match them.
  • Send only what the service lists. Some services accept the control module alone; others require the complete pump and module assembly. The "What to send" section on your service page is the authority.
  • Photograph the module label before packing, so you have the part number on hand if we need to talk.
  • Removal. Procedures vary by vehicle. [REMOVAL GUIDANCE — state whether you provide removal notes, and advise using a qualified shop where brake lines must be opened]

Packaging

  1. Use a rigid cardboard box with at least 5 cm (2 in) of space around the module on every side. Never ship in an envelope or padded mailer, even for a small controller.
  2. Protect the connector. Cover the electrical connector so pins cannot be bent. If you have an anti-static bag, place the controller in it.
  3. Complete assemblies: cap or plug every brake-line port to keep fluid in and debris out, then bag the unit and surround it with absorbent packing. Fluid that leaks in transit damages the controller and the carton.
  4. Fill all voids with bubble wrap, foam, or crumpled paper so the module cannot shift. Shake the closed box gently — nothing should move.
  5. Tape all seams with packing tape. Do not reuse a box that is crushed or soft.

Do not disassemble the controller from the hydraulic block, open the module housing, or remove any component. Doing so can make a repairable module unrepairable and may void eligibility.

What to include in the box

  • A note or printed order confirmation with your order number, name, phone, email, and return shipping address.
  • The module (or assembly) exactly as the service specifies.
  • Optional: a short description of the symptoms and the fault codes you read, if they differ from what you entered at checkout.

Do not include

  • Brackets, bolts, wiring harnesses, or brake lines unless the service page asks for them.
  • Loose parts or hardware in the same box as the module.
  • Payment, cash, or cheques — all payment is through the store.
