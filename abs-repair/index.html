Is it the module?

Common signs of a failed ABS module

Wheel speed sensors and wiring fail too — but if you see these symptoms together with the codes below, the control module is the most likely cause.

⚠️

ABS / Brake / Traction lights

Warning lights that stay on, or come and go with bumps, heat, or cold starts.

📉

Speedometer drops out

On many trucks the ABS module supplies vehicle speed — a bad module can kill the speedometer and cruise control.

🔌

No communication

Scan tool can't talk to the ABS module, or you see U-codes for lost communication with the ABS/EBCM.

🛑

Pump runs nonstop or never

ABS pump motor won't shut off, or never runs during self-test. Often a failed relay or driver on the circuit board.

🧊

Intermittent faults

Cracked solder joints from heat cycling cause faults that appear and disappear — classic module failure.

🧾

Failed inspection

An ABS warning lamp is a fail item in many states. A repair gets you back on the road quickly.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

How do I know the module is the problem and not a sensor?

Pull the codes with a scan tool that reads ABS. Pump motor, relay, valve, and internal-fault codes usually point to the module. Wheel speed sensor codes usually point to a sensor or wiring. Send us your codes and we'll tell you honestly whether a module repair makes sense before you ship anything.

Do I need to bleed the brakes or open brake lines?

On most GM, Ford and many other vehicles, the electronic module separates from the hydraulic unit with a few screws — the brake lines stay connected and no bleeding is needed. A few vehicles require removing the full unit; we'll tell you when you request a quote.

Will the module need to be programmed after repair?

No. We repair your original module, so it keeps its original VIN and configuration. Reinstall it, clear the codes, and drive.

How long does it take?

Standard repairs are completed within 1–2 business day of arrival; rush repairs go out the same day. Total door-to-door time is usually 4–7 days depending on shipping.

What does the warranty cover?

Our Lifetime warranty covers the repair we performed. If the same fault returns, send it back and we'll fix it at no charge. Physical damage, water intrusion, and incorrect installation are not covered.

What if you can't fix it?

If the module is unrepairable you only pay the $45 evaluation fee and we return your module, or we can quote a tested replacement.
